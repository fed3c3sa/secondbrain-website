How SecondBrain works, in three minutes

By Federico Cesarini, maker of SecondBrain · 3:14 · Watch on YouTube

Federico Cesarini, who built SecondBrain, shows the app from his phone. He double taps the back of the phone, talks, and the note lands in the right folder with the event on his calendar. Then he connects the same memory to Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, so every assistant he uses knows what he told the app.

What the video covers

  • Why he built it: too many ideas and projects, and no way to keep track of them.
  • Capture: with Back Tap, a double tap on the back of the iPhone opens SecondBrain and starts recording.
  • Recall: ask what is on today, or about something you said a month ago, and it answers from your notes and calendar.
  • Shared memory: Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini read and write the same notes over MCP, so you have one brain across all your AIs.
