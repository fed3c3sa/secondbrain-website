What is a second brain app? Che cos'è un'app second brain?

A second brain app is a tool that captures everything you want to remember and stores it so you can recall it instantly. SecondBrain does this with your voice: you speak, and it turns what you said into organized notes, calendar events and reminders, then keeps them ready across your devices and AI assistants.

Un'app second brain è uno strumento che cattura tutto ciò che vuoi ricordare e lo conserva così da poterlo richiamare in un istante. SecondBrain lo fa con la tua voce: parli, e trasforma ciò che hai detto in note organizzate, eventi in calendario e promemoria, poi li tiene pronti su tutti i tuoi dispositivi e su tutte le tue AI.

What is SecondBrain? Che cos'è SecondBrain?

SecondBrain is an AI second brain app for iPhone and Android. You speak once and it remembers: it writes structured notes, files them, and creates calendar events and follow-ups while you talk. It also gives your AI assistants a shared long-term memory, so what you tell one is known by them all.

SecondBrain è un'app second brain con AI per iPhone e Android. Parli una volta e lui ricorda: scrive note strutturate, le archivia e crea eventi in calendario e promemoria mentre parli. Dà inoltre ai tuoi assistenti AI una memoria condivisa a lungo termine, così ciò che dici a uno lo sanno tutti.

Sì, con dei limiti. L'app SecondBrain è gratuita da scaricare e usare su iPhone, Android e sul web: con il piano gratuito catturi, organizzi e ritrovi le tue note. How does SecondBrain share memory across ChatGPT, Claude and other AI assistants? Come condivide SecondBrain la memoria tra ChatGPT, Claude e gli altri assistenti AI?

SecondBrain connects to your assistants through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Tell something to Claude, ChatGPT or Claude Code once and it lands in a single shared memory that is yours. Every assistant you connect can then recall it, on any device, without you repeating yourself.

SecondBrain si collega ai tuoi assistenti tramite il Model Context Protocol (MCP). Dici qualcosa a Claude, ChatGPT o Claude Code una volta e finisce in un'unica memoria condivisa, la tua. Ogni assistente che colleghi può poi richiamarla, su qualsiasi dispositivo, senza che tu lo ripeta.

How does the Model Context Protocol (MCP) connection work? Come funziona il collegamento tramite Model Context Protocol (MCP)?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is an open standard that lets AI assistants call external tools and data sources. SecondBrain runs an MCP server that your assistant connects to after you sign in with Apple or Google, with no API keys to copy. Once connected, the assistant can save to and recall from your SecondBrain memory during a chat, and that same memory is shared across every assistant you connect.

Il Model Context Protocol (MCP) è uno standard aperto che permette agli assistenti AI di usare strumenti e fonti di dati esterni. SecondBrain espone un server MCP a cui il tuo assistente si collega dopo l'accesso con Apple o Google, senza chiavi API da copiare. Una volta collegato, l'assistente può salvare e richiamare la tua memoria SecondBrain durante la chat, e quella stessa memoria è condivisa con ogni assistente che colleghi.

Where is my data stored? Dove sono salvati i miei dati?

SecondBrain stores your notes, folders and calendar events in a Supabase PostgreSQL database hosted in the European Union, isolated per account with Row-Level Security so you can only reach your own data. Voice and text are processed in real time by Google Gemini on its paid API tier, which does not train on your content. Everything travels over HTTPS, and you can delete your account and all its data at any time from the app.

SecondBrain salva note, cartelle ed eventi di calendario in un database PostgreSQL su Supabase, ospitato nell'Unione Europea e isolato per account tramite Row-Level Security: puoi accedere solo ai tuoi dati. Voce e testo vengono elaborati in tempo reale da Google Gemini (API a pagamento), che non addestra i modelli sui tuoi contenuti. Tutto viaggia su HTTPS e puoi eliminare l'account con tutti i dati quando vuoi, direttamente dall'app.

Privacy Policy

How SecondBrain handles your data.

Last updated · July 13, 2026

SecondBrain ("we", "our", or "the app") is an AI-powered note-taking assistant operated by Influpress LLC, a Wyoming (USA) limited liability company with registered office at 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the app's features:

  • Account information, your email address and display name, collected during sign-up via Google, Apple, or email and password authentication.
  • User-generated content, the notes, folders, summaries, and calendar events you create within the app.
  • Voice recordings, audio you record is processed in real time to create notes. Recordings are sent to Google Gemini for transcription and are not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Timezone, used to correctly schedule calendar events.
  • Advertising and usage identifiers, only if you agree to the App Tracking prompt on your device. In that case a small set of app events (such as install and app open) and your device advertising identifier are shared with Meta so we can measure whether our ads bring people to the app. If you decline the prompt, no advertising identifier is collected and no cross-app tracking takes place. Your notes, voice, and calendar are never part of this.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • AI processing, your text and audio are sent to Google Gemini (paid API tier) to generate notes, summaries, and calendar events. Your content is processed in real time, is not used to train AI models, and is retained by Google only briefly for abuse and safety monitoring, per Google's paid API terms.
  • Storage, your notes, folders, and calendar events are stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database hosted in the European Union, isolated by user account via Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Authentication, we use Supabase Auth with Google OAuth and Apple Sign-In to securely manage your account.
  • Advertising measurement, if you allow tracking, Meta's SDK reports coarse app events so we can tell which ads work. It never receives your notes, voice, or calendar.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following services to operate the app:

  • Google Gemini, AI processing of voice and text (paid API tier, no training on your data).
